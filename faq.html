Frequently Asked Questions

For Candidates

Is APEX completely free for job seekers?

Yes. Creating a profile, browsing jobs, and applying to positions is 100% free for all candidates. We only charge companies to post their open roles.

How do I know if a job listing is active?

We enforce strict expiration policies. Jobs are automatically removed after 30 days unless explicitly renewed by the employer[cite: 1]. If you see it on the board, they are still accepting applications.

Can I filter for only remote jobs?

Absolutely. You can use the quick filters on the homepage to instantly isolate fully remote positions, or visit our dedicated Work From Home portal for exclusively remote roles[cite: 2, 4].

Who sees my application data?

Your data is only shared with the specific employer you submit an application to. We do not sell candidate data to third-party recruiters or agencies.

For Employers

How long does a job post stay active?

A standard job post remains active and fully searchable for 30 days. You can choose to renew or pause your listing at any time from your employer dashboard.

Are salary ranges mandatory?

While not strictly mandatory for every single role, we highly encourage salary transparency[cite: 1]. Listings with clear salary bands receive significantly higher application rates and better candidate alignment.

Does APEX integrate with my ATS?

Yes. Customers on our Growth and Enterprise plans can integrate APEX applications directly into Greenhouse, Lever, and Workable workflows.

How do you verify candidates?

Candidates are required to verify their email addresses upon signup. We also run automated checks to filter out bot submissions, ensuring the applications hitting your dashboard are from real professionals.
